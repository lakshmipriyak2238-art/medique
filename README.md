🏥 MediQue

Turning Hospital Waiting into Organized Care.

«What if waiting at a hospital didn't have to be confusing?»

MediQue is a web-based Hospital OPD Queue Management System designed to simplify patient visits through digital token booking, queue tracking, doctor availability updates and emergency-priority handling.

🌐 Live Demo: "Visit MediQue" (https://medique-sigma.vercel.app)

---

💡 The Problem

Hospital OPDs often face:

- Long waiting queues and overcrowding.
- Uncertainty about doctor availability.
- Difficulty tracking patient turns.
- Challenges in managing emergency cases.

🚀 Our Solution

MediQue brings patient token booking and OPD queue management into one simple digital platform, helping patients track their turns and staff organize queues more efficiently.

✨ Key Features

Patient Features

- Digital token booking.
- Queue progress tracking.
- Doctor profiles and availability.
- Seven OPD department options.
- English, Tamil and Hindi support.

Staff Features

- Active queue management.
- Call Next Patient controls.
- Emergency-priority handling.
- Doctor availability updates.
- Browser-based audio alerts.
